HDP Office App: Terms of Use and Privacy

The HDP Office App is an internal tool of Horizon Defense Project, a Michigan nonprofit. It connects only to HDP's own QuickBooks Online company, and only HDP uses it. It is not offered to anyone else, and no license is granted to anyone outside HDP. It is provided as is.

Privacy. The app reads and records HDP's own bookkeeping data so HDP's books stay current. That includes an AI assistant HDP uses for office administration. It collects no data from anyone else. It never sells or shares data and never uses it for advertising. Its access to QuickBooks is stored encrypted on HDP's own computers, and HDP can disconnect it at any time in QuickBooks (Settings → Apps).
